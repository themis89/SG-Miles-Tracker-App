How this works

Each round shows you a kana and four romaji options. Get one right and it moves up a "box"; get it wrong and it drops back — so struggling characters resurface sooner and mastered ones fade out. That's spaced repetition, and it's why five focused minutes a day beats an hour of passive reading.

  • New characters are introduced gradually, a few per session.
  • Characters "due" for review are prioritized automatically.
  • Every few days (randomly, so it stays a genuine test) a longer Surprise Test pulls from everything you've learned so far and logs your score over time.
